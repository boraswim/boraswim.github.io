TierFight

A multiplayer tier-list party game. Friends join a room with a six-character code and take turns moving cards between the S to F tiers until the rounds run out, ending up with one shared (and hotly disputed) tier list.

Play at tierfight.com

The game

  • Jump straight in: 2 to 12 players, no account needed. The host picks a collection, the turn timer and the number of rounds.
  • Every turn counts: swap two cards, bring one in from storage, send one back, or skip, with drag and drop, clicks or the keyboard.
  • Powerups: one single-use powerup each per game, such as Lock, Banish, Mute, Shuffle, New Tier and New Item.
  • Room chat and a new game button that keeps the lobby together; the finished board exports as a PNG.
  • Collections: built-in sets like Turkish Desserts and World Street Foods using freely licensed photos, plus player-made collections reviewed by moderators.
  • For everyone: 11 languages including right-to-left Arabic, light and dark mode, music and sound effects.

Under the hood

  • React on Vinext, served by a single Cloudflare Worker with a D1 (SQLite) database and Drizzle migrations.
  • Google sign-in for creating collections, with hashed session cookies and a moderation queue.
  • Automated tests run on every deploy, and pushes to main deploy automatically.
