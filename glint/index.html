Glint

A media player for Android TV that turns the films and series on your NAS into a polished library: posters, artwork, plots, cast, trailers and ratings, with playback built for home cinema.

Source on GitHub Privacy policy

What it does

  • Plays your own files: straight from your network shares (SMB, WebDAV, FTP, FTPS, SFTP, NFS and UPnP/DLNA) and USB disks, with just the folders you pick. An older NAS can use a fixed SMB version.
  • A library that looks the part: films, series with seasons and episodes, collections, genres and folders, with details, artwork and trailers from TMDB, and IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes scores through OMDb, using your own free keys. Local .nfo files and artwork next to your files are used too.
  • Playback for home cinema: 4K and HDR with the TV switched to the film's frame rate, lossless multichannel audio sent to your receiver untouched, resume points, chapters, intro and credits skipping, seek previews, and subtitle and audio timing.
  • Easy to get around with a remote: an A–Z dock and filters for unwatched, 4K, HDR and more in big libraries, and a Watching row that picks up where you left off.
  • For everyone at home: profiles with age limits and a PIN, watchlist, playlists and your own collections, in 10 interface languages.
  • Private by design: no account, no server, no ads and no tracking. Your library and settings stay on your TV.
